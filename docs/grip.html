Devil's Grip

Two packs, aces out · 96 cards

How to play
  • Two packs with the aces taken out make 96 cards. Twenty-four are dealt face up in three rows of eight. The rest is the stock.
  • Every pile builds up in steps of three, in one suit. Top row: 2 · 5 · 8 · J. Middle row: 3 · 6 · 9 · Q. Bottom row: 4 · 7 · 10 · K.
  • Tap a card, then tap a pile. If the card is the same suit and three higher, it builds on top. Piles only build in their own row, so an 8 can only be built in the top row.
  • If the card is three lower than a pile's bottom card, it slides underneath and the pile stays where it is. A whole pile can slide under, or move on top of, another pile the same way.
  • Tapping a pile where the card cannot build swaps the two piles. Swaps are how you move cards into their rows. A single card can swap anywhere, but a stack of two or more cards always stays in its own row.
  • Undo takes back moves since your last deal or fill. Once a card is turned up from the stock it stays, just like with real cards.
  • Sort tidies every row: finished piles move to the left and piles from other rows move to the right. It counts as one move and can be undone.
  • Hints outlines every spot the selected card can go. It starts off.
  • Empty spaces fill only from the stock, never from the waste. That is the only way new 2s, 3s and 4s join the grid. If the stock is empty, turn the waste over first. With Auto-fill on they fill straight away. With it off, a space stays open so you can move piles into it first, and you tap the space when you want it filled. Turning auto-fill back on fills any open spaces.
  • Tap the stock to deal three cards to the waste. Only the top waste card can be played. When the stock runs out, tap it again to turn the waste over. There is no limit on redeals.
  • When you are stuck, end the game. Your score is the cards left in the stock and waste: 10 or fewer is good, 5 or fewer excellent, 2 or fewer brilliant. Getting all 96 home is rare.
