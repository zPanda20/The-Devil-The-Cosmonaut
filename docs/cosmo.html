Cosmonaut Sudoku

Place the tokens: one in every row, column and region, never touching

More

  • Tap a cell to mark an X. Tap it again to clear it.
  • Double tap a cell to place a token. On a keyboard, press S on a cell.
  • Every row, column and coloured region needs exactly one token. Tokens can never touch, not even diagonally.
  • A token in the wrong place costs a life. Lose all three and the mission fails: you lose points and restart the level.
  • Undo takes back X marks. Placed tokens stay.
