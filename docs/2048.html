How to play
  • Swipe, or use the arrow keys (or W A S D), to slide every tile. Two matching tiles that collide merge into one worth double.
  • A new 2 or 4 appears after every move. Reach the 2048 tile and it turns into your deck's King; keep going for higher tiles.
  • The game ends when the board is full and nothing can merge. Points are paid when a game ends, plus a bonus the moment you reach 2048.
