How to play
  • Remove pairs of cards that add up to 13. Aces count 1, Jacks 11, Queens 12. Kings are 13 on their own: tap one to remove it.
  • Only uncovered cards can be used: a pyramid card is free once both cards on top of it are gone. The top of the waste is always free.
  • Tap the stock to turn a card onto the waste. When the stock runs out you can turn the waste over, up to the number of passes in More.
  • Clear the whole pyramid to win. Undo cannot reach back past a card turned from the stock.
