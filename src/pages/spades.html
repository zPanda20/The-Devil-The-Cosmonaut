How to play
  • You and Baron (across) play as partners against Lilith and Mephisto. Spades are always trumps.
  • After the deal, everyone bids how many tricks they expect to take. Your bid and your partner's add up to your team's contract.
  • Follow suit if you can; otherwise play anything, including a spade to trump. Spades cannot be led until one has been played (or you hold only spades).
  • Make your contract: 10 points per trick bid, plus 1 per extra trick (a bag). Miss it: lose 10 per trick bid. Every 10 bags costs 100 points.
  • Nil is a bid of zero tricks: +100 if you take none, −100 if you take any. Your partner's bid still counts on its own.
  • First team to 500 wins (a team that falls to −200 loses). Tap a card to raise it, tap again to play; Quick play in More plays on one tap.
