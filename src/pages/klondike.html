How to play
  • Build the four foundations up by suit from Ace to King. Build the seven columns down in alternating colours.
  • Tap a card to send it to the best place: a foundation if it can go, otherwise a column. Drag a card or a run to put it exactly where you want.
  • Only a King (or a run starting with a King) can fill an empty column.
  • Tap the stock to turn one or three cards (set in More). Turn the waste over as often as you like.
  • Winnable deals (in More) only deals games a solver has already won, so every deal can be cleared.
  • Undo cannot reach back past a card turned from the stock or a card turned face up.
