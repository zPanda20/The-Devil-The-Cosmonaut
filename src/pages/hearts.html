How to play
  • Four players, thirteen cards each. Every heart you take costs 1 point and the Queen of Spades costs 13. Lowest score wins when someone reaches 100.
  • Before each hand, pass three cards: left, then right, then across, then no pass, and round again.
  • The 2 of Clubs leads the first trick. Follow suit if you can; otherwise play anything. No hearts or Queen of Spades on the first trick unless you have nothing else.
  • Hearts cannot be led until one has been played on another suit (or you hold nothing but hearts).
  • Take all 13 hearts and the Queen and you shoot the moon: everyone else takes 26 instead.
  • Tap a card to raise it, tap it again to play. Turn on Quick play in More to play with a single tap.
